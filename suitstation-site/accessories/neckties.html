Neckties in Gainesville at Suit Station.

The necktie is the easiest accessory to get right and the easiest to get wrong. Width matters — between 2.5 and 3 inches at the widest point reads modern; anything skinnier reads last-decade; anything wider than 3.25 reads outdated 1990s. Most Hall County office and wedding looks land right around 2.75 inches. We pull ties in the right width range against the suit and shirt at the fitting on Pearl Nix Pkwy so the proportion reads intentional on the customer's frame instead of guessed at later from a department store rack.

Neckties in Gainesville at Suit Station cover the full range. Silk ties for business, weddings, funerals, and traditional events. Knit ties for business-creative offices and smart-casual contexts. For groomsmen, all ties same color across the party for the line-up photo. For the groom, a slight variation reads intentional.
