What we do

Men's Shoes at Suit Station.

A suit fails without the shoes. We see it constantly — guys spend $400 on a suit and pair it with the cheap dress shoes they bought in college, and the whole look reads off. Real leather dress shoes do three things a $40 pair cannot: they fit the foot properly, they take a polish and hold a shine, and they age the right way (better with use, not worse).

Our shoe section covers six categories: men's dress shoes (oxfords, derbys, captoes) at $65, loafers (penny, tassel, bit, horsebit, velvet) at $45, wedding shoes (captoe oxfords, patent leather for tuxedos) at $65, prom shoes (slim-toe leather, velvet loafers) at $45 to $65 depending on style, formal shoes (patent leather oxford for tuxedo wear) at $65, and kids shoes (boys' dress shoes $65, kids' loafers $45). We stock real leather across all categories and avoid the synthetic uppers that crack within a year. Sizes stocked through standard adult ranges and youth dress sizing for ring bearers and junior groomsmen. Color range: black, dark brown, oxblood, tan suede, and patent leather (formal only). Care services available at the counter; resoling referral to a local cobbler.
