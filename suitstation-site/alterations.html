What we do

Alterations & Tailoring at Suit Station.

Most "tailors" in north Georgia are dry cleaners with a sewing machine in the back. We are the opposite — full alterations and tailoring department staffed by master tailors who do nothing but fit and finish menswear, women's formalwear, and kids' clothing. If a garment can be altered to fit better, we can do it. If it cannot, we will tell you upfront before charging.

Our alterations department covers five categories: suit alterations (jackets and trousers), dress shirt alterations (collars, sleeves, body taper), formal wear alterations (tuxedos, dinner jackets, ceremonial dress), men's clothing alterations (dress pants, sport coats, outerwear), and kids' suit alterations (boys' suits with growth allowance). Standard turn is 3 to 5 business days for most alterations, with same-day rush available on hems and waist work for $10 above standard. Walk-ins welcome; appointments preferred for full re-fits or wedding party work. Pricing is line-item — we quote each alteration before you commit and never bundle in surprise charges. Master tailors on staff have 15+ years of experience each and can handle complex jobs (lapel re-shaping, jacket re-balancing, sleeve work on outerwear) that most local shops will turn down.

#1: Measured, Marked, Stitched On-Site

Every step happens at 150 Pearl Nix Pkwy. We pin and chalk you in the fitting room, mark the seam allowance with the garment on, then hand-finish where it matters (hems, sleeves with buttonholes) and machine the rest. The suit goes back on you for a second look before pickup — no work shipped out, no third-party tailors.

#2: Line-Item Pricing, Quoted Before We Cut

Pant hem $18. Waist taper $24. Sleeve length $25 to $45 depending on whether the buttonholes need to be moved. Jacket sides $40 to $80. You see the per-item cost and the total before we touch the suit, and you only pay for the work you actually need — no $50 minimums on a single hem.

#3: 3–5 Days Standard, Same-Day Rush Available

Standard turn on most jobs is 3 to 5 business days. Our $10 same-day rush moves you to the front of the queue on hems and waist work — walk in by 11 AM and pick up by close. Wedding party fittings and complex re-fits run 1 to 2 weeks; we set the timeline at the first fitting so you can plan around it.
