What we do

Men's Accessories at Suit Station.

Accessories are where most outfits live or die. A great suit with a wrong tie reads off. A modest suit with a sharp pocket square reads polished. We stock accessories deep — dress shirts in 28 colors and patterns, neckties and bowties across 200+ designs, belts and shoe leather coordinated, cufflinks and suspenders for full formal kits. Bring your suit and we coordinate the full set in one visit.

Six core accessory categories at Suit Station. Long-sleeve dress shirts: white, blue, and patterned dress shirts in modern and classic fits, plus formal pleated for tuxedo wear. Bow ties: hand-tied silk bow ties for true black-tie, velvet bow ties for creative gala, patterned bow ties for weddings and prom-creative. Neckties: silk, wool, and knit ties in modern 2.5 to 3 inch widths, solid, striped, and patterned. Belts: leather dress belts in black and dark brown matched to shoe color. Suspenders: button-end suspenders for tuxedo trousers and three-piece suits, interior trouser buttons added at no charge. Cufflinks: classic onyx-and-mother-of-pearl stud sets for tuxedos, silver and gold knot cufflinks for French-cuff dress shirts. We coordinate the full kit at the fitting on Pearl Nix Pkwy and pull accessories alongside the suit so the whole look comes together cleanly.
